# Live place discovery

The noncommercial class project uses two read-only, no-key services. Searches happen only after a visitor submits a city/state or five-digit US ZIP and then requests nearby places. There is no GPS collection, home-address field, autocomplete, account, booking, or paid service.

- [Open-Meteo geocoding](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/geocoding-api) resolves a city or ZIP center. Multiple matches remain separate choices; the app does not silently pick a different state. The provider supports a state qualifier, including `Paterson, NJ`, and the request is filtered to US results. Show attribution to Open-Meteo / GeoNames beside results.
- [Overpass by Private.coffee](https://overpass.private.coffee/) reads named OpenStreetMap restaurants, dessert shops, parks, thrift/charity/antique/book shops, museums, bowling alleys, arcades and entertainment venues. This operator explicitly allows application use without registration or paid access. Show [OpenStreetMap attribution and its license link](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). Results are a limited sample within a straight-line radius, not an exhaustive directory or driving-distance estimate.

These are source-linked community listings, **not independently verified businesses**. Data may be incomplete or outdated. A street address appears only when the source supplies a house number, street, and city or postal code. Otherwise the app may direct Maps to the listed coordinates and says the address is unavailable. Do not invent hours, availability, admission fees or current menu prices. Even an OSM `fee=no` tag does not become a verified $0 price. All live entries have `priceKnown:false`, `costCents:null`, `hoursKnown:false` and `availabilityKnown:false`. Keep discovery separate from the budgeted catalog until a reliable price is supplied; any visitor estimate must be labeled as their estimate rather than a verified venue price.

## Limits and production suitability

Open-Meteo's [free terms](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms) permit noncommercial use below 10,000 requests/day, 5,000/hour and 600/minute, with attribution and no uptime guarantee. Commercial distribution needs a compliant geocoding-provider arrangement; this project does not enable a paid plan. [Private.coffee's own terms and privacy policy](https://overpass.private.coffee/) allow any project, including commercial use, with fair resource sharing, meaningful identification and no private information in queries. They prefer POST and request advance notification of large operations (more than 10 requests/second or 10 concurrent requests). No uptime warranty is offered. Provider access may end at any time. This explicit-submit integration supports a modest classroom prototype, not evidence of App Store-scale reliability.

The implementation caches geocoding for 24 hours and nearby samples for 6 hours, coalesces identical in-flight requests, bounds the cache to 120 entries, and rejects new provider requests less than 1.5 seconds apart. These are per-process guards. Vercel's separate instances do not share the cache or throttle; they are not a global quota guarantee. Provider failures are readable 429/503 errors while curated ideas remain usable. Do not automatically retry or run background searches. Request timeouts, Overpass runtime/memory/output limits, and streamed response-byte limits bound resource use. Nearby discovery returns at most 60 entries.

`GEOCODING_URL` and `OVERPASS_URL` may replace the HTTPS endpoints through server environment variables without changing browser code. Do not configure a paid endpoint without approval. Recheck terms, expected traffic and attribution before a wider launch. Private.coffee may temporarily log truncated requester IP addresses and request metadata for up to 48 hours, as its linked privacy policy explains. The shared server makes provider requests; no visitor account or precise device location is transmitted. The main `overpass-api.de` and public Nominatim endpoints are intentionally unused: their policies include restrictions on app and automatically generated integrations; this deployment does not depend on exemptions from those restrictions.

## Integration and validation

Instantiate `createLocationSearch()` once per server process. Use `geocode(query)` for `GET /api/locations?q=...`, and `nearby({lat,lon,radius,kind})` for a separate explicit `GET /api/nearby` request. Convert validated query-string numbers at the route boundary; reject missing, repeated, blank, unexpected and nonfinite parameters. Errors expose a readable message and `status` (400, 429 or 503). Avoid logging raw location queries.

`test/location-search.test.js` exercises city/ZIP validation, ambiguity, attribution, duplicate-request/cache isolation, fixed query construction, sourced metadata, missing/unsafe URLs, radius bounds, unknown prices, rate limits, timeouts, oversized responses and provider failures using injected HTTP responses. These deterministic tests do not claim the public providers were available on a deployed phone.

## Curated-first discovery and price confidence

Use lib/curated-nearby.js for the fast path: curatedNearby(options, townData, {lat,lon,radius,kind}, {now,limit}) is synchronous and requires no external HTTP call. Options are the checked local catalog; townData is the recorded municipality representative-point table. The helper validates the same location, radius and category values as live discovery, excludes entries with missing sources or inconsistent price arithmetic, groups menu variants by category and venue, and returns at most 60 cards by default (120 maximum). An empty result outside recorded NJ coverage stays empty; the code never invents nearby businesses or assigns unknown prices to zero.

Recommended flow: render curated source-priced choices immediately from a separate /api/nearby-curated route. A visitor can then explicitly request broader community map listings. Keep already displayed curated choices when that live request fails; say that curated results are still available and the live provider could not be reached. No automatic retry or second donated endpoint is required. This separates dependable local results from optional live-service availability.

Each curated result exposes the original optionIds, minCostCents/maxCostCents across checked sample plans, the underlying priceExamples, original source links, recorded check date, estimated tax/tip/donation components, and exclusions. A checked range describes those examples, not every menu item or a guaranteed current bill. Directions target the recorded street address; distance uses the town's representative point and must be labeled accordingly. No result claims live opening hours, admission availability or an exact driving distance.

Freshness is calculated from the stored checkedOn date, never by writing today's date during a search. A group's oldest sample-check date is used. Checks older than 30 days carry review-due as a maintenance reminder; this threshold is an app convention, not a freshness guarantee. Missing, malformed or future dates need reconfirmation. Even a same-day check says to reconfirm before visiting.

A small source spot-check on November 19, 2026 confirmed existing samples without changing their catalog prices or dates: Kafe Neo's official menu lists the Grilled Chicken Avocado Club panini at $15 (https://thekafeneo.com/menu/); Sook's official location-specific page lists Turkey & Swiss at $12 (https://www.sookpastry.com/menu/sandwiches-soup?location_id=3617); Paterson Museum's official page lists a suggested $2 adult donation and under-18 admission free (https://patersonmuseum.com/). These checks cover those examples only, not the entire catalog or future availability. The museum also warns it may close unexpectedly in high temperatures; visitors should confirm directly.


## Local NJ city/ZIP resolution - November 20, 2026

The known North Jersey search no longer depends on a live geocoding call. data/location-index.json contains all 43 supported municipalities and 102 distinct ZIP codes associated through exact nearby NJ GeoNames populated-place records and existing source-linked public venue addresses. Each postal association keeps its evidence. Coordinates exactly retain the existing Census municipality representative points; they are not ZIP centroids, street addresses or measured drive times. Shared ZIPs preserve multiple municipality choices, such as 07424 for Little Falls and Woodland Park.

GET /api/options exposes a compact local index for on-device suggestions. Typing uses only that local index and sends no autocomplete requests to third parties. Enter or Find location submits the lookup; a unique match is applied and source-priced nearby choices load automatically. Multiple matches require selection. Local prefix suggestions are never silently treated as exact server matches, and explicit non-NJ qualifiers are left to the national lookup. Unknown cities/ZIPs retain the original explicit-submit Open-Meteo fallback.

City lookup and place lookup have separate cancellation/version guards. Starting a new city request cancels old place work, and obsolete responses cannot replace the latest location/results. Changing the town, radius or category refreshes the displayed source-priced choices. GeoNames/Census attribution remains visible. Offline ZIP association availability does not imply live business availability, phone verification or offline date generation.
